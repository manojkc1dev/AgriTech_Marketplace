# Business Requirements Document (BRD)

## 1. Primary Mission

The AgriTech Platform provides a secure, traceable, multi-sided marketplace and supply chain management system connecting agricultural producers directly with commercial buyers, backed by transparent logistics and governance.

## 2. Strategic Objectives

- Eliminate intermediate margin extraction by enabling direct transactions between farmers/cooperatives and buyers.
- Enforce strict quality grading and origin batch traceability across the supply chain.
- Streamline cooperative collection, member aggregation, and payment transparency.
- Provide secure, enterprise-grade governance through explicit KYC enforcement and administrative role separation.

## 3. Stakeholder Breakdown

- **Producers (Farmers & Cooperatives):** Seek fair pricing, predictable pickup schedules, and access to commercial buyers.
- **Buyers (Wholesalers, Processors, Retailers):** Require guaranteed quality standards, transparent delivery tracking, and formal negotiation mechanisms.
- **Logistics Operators (Managers & Delivery Agents):** Need centralized dispatching, route clarity, hub tracking, and proof-of-delivery tools.
- **Platform Administrators:** Require granular system auditing, KYC verification tools, privilege protection, and platform configuration oversight.
