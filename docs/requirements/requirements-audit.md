## Open Business Decisions

### OBD-001 — Cooperative Registration Approval

**Current proposed rule:**

Cooperative Manager approves cooperative registration.

**Problem:**

The initial Cooperative Manager must itself be created or authorized before the manager can approve/manage a cooperative.

**Decision required:**

Who creates and approves the first Cooperative Manager?

**Proposed model under consideration:**

Cooperative onboarding
↓
Admin/Super Admin creates or approves Cooperative Manager
↓
Cooperative Manager becomes authorized
↓
Cooperative Manager manages cooperative onboarding

**Questions requiring business confirmation:**

1. Who creates the first Cooperative Manager?
2. Who approves the Cooperative Manager?
3. Who approves the initial Cooperative?
4. Can Super Admin override/review cooperative approval?
5. Can Admin approve cooperatives?
6. Can one Cooperative Manager manage multiple cooperatives?
7. Does the Cooperative Manager approve only cooperatives assigned to them?

**Status:** OPEN

**Blocking:** Cooperative onboarding implementation
